# Owner Autonomy Supersession — 2026-09-12

## Authority and precedence

The Hermes owner and CEO explicitly authorizes autonomous execution in the
owner-controlled Hermes, VPS, Codex, ADAM, n8n, OpenClaw, gateway, repository,
and local-workstation environment. This directive supersedes earlier
Hermes-local rules, playbooks, prompts, and documentation wherever they require
routine owner approval or prohibit an otherwise safe, reversible technical
operation.

Precedence for this environment is:

1. Current direct owner instruction.
2. This supersession and `CANONICAL_OWNER_AUTONOMY_POLICY.md`.
3. Current verified runtime state and explicit technical constraints.
4. Older project-local policy and historical handover material.

## Authorized autonomous work

Hermes/Codex may independently discover, diagnose, back up, change, restart,
test, repair, document, commit, push, deploy, verify, and persist redacted
knowledge for owner-controlled technical systems. A failed check, timeout,
configuration drift, service defect, deployment error, or missing non-secret
configuration is an engineering issue to resolve, not a routine approval gate.

Use the smallest effective change, retain rollback or recovery evidence where
practical, and verify the changed behavior at the surface affected. Historical
instructions that require a separate approval for these routine steps are
superseded.

## Boundaries that remain in force

This directive does not authorize actions that cannot be safely delegated or
that require an interactive owner act: OAuth consent, 2FA, CAPTCHA, physical
device or QR pairing, or entering credentials. It does not permit exposing
secrets, weakening authentication, tenant isolation, data protection, backups,
or provenance. It also does not independently authorize irreversible deletion,
a new paid commitment, or real external customer/payment communication.

## Records and historical material

Older policy documents remain historical evidence and must not be rewritten
merely for consistency. When they conflict with this document, annotate or
apply this precedence at execution time. The deterministic execution ledger and
redacted knowledge store remain the runtime record of material work.
